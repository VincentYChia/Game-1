Async work (LLM items, NPC dialogue, WMS L2 upgrades) runs on daemon threads and is polled back on the main thread — no LLM ever stalls the frame.

  • Crash‑guarded: a bad frame logs & continues; 5 in a row → emergency save + clean exit.
  • Persistence is event‑driven (autosave on quit), not per‑frame.

10 · WNS — Narrative · the depth exemplar

How WNS threads are coordinated & searched

  • Match or mint: new fragment's tags vs recent clusters at the same address by Jaccard ≥ 0.5, 1‑week window → join or mint. thread_index.py:113
  • Deterministic id: SHA‑256 of (address + sorted tags + first_seen) — the LLM never invents IDs.
  • Vocabulary accumulates: a cluster's tags are the union of its members; divergence splits threads.
  • Cross‑layer promotion: parent_thread_id lifts a village arc into district/region/world.
  • Search: query_by_address + extract_active_threads (newest per thread, capped).
Continuity — up & down

Up (C3): NL3+ pulls lower context from child addresses — a district is caused by its villages.

Down (M1): NL7 world currents cascade into NL2‑6 as ${world_framing}.
